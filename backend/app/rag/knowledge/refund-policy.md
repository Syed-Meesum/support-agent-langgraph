# Refund Policy

## Overview

Customers may request a refund for a charge they believe was made in error. Every
refund goes through the policy engine before it is executed. The agent proposes an
amount; policy decides whether it may be actioned automatically, needs a human, or
is refused.

## Auto-approval threshold

Refunds of 50.00 or less that are supported by charge evidence are auto-approved.
Refunds above 50.00 and up to 500.00 require human review. Refunds above 500.00
always require human review, with no exceptions.

## Eligibility window

A charge is eligible for refund consideration for 90 days after the charge date.
Charges older than 90 days are escalated rather than refunded automatically.

## Evidence requirement

A refund is only considered when the charge record itself supports it: the charge
must exist, belong to the customer, and have a status of succeeded. A refund is
never issued against a failed or already refunded charge.
