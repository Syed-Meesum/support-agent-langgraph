# Billing FAQ

## Can I get a refund for a renewal I forgot to cancel?

A renewal that was charged while the subscription was active is treated as a normal
refund request. It is assessed against the refund policy, including the amount
thresholds, and is not automatically granted.

## How long do refunds take?

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. The refund is recorded
immediately; the money typically returns to the customer's account within 5 to 10
business days depending on the bank.

## Why was I charged twice?

The most common cause is a retried payment: the first attempt appears to fail, the
system retries, and both attempts settle. See the duplicate charge policy for how
this is refunded.

## Can I cancel and get the current month refunded?

Yes, both actions can be requested, but they are handled separately: the
cancellation stops the next renewal, and the refund for the current period is
assessed on its own merits.

## What if my account is suspended?

Actions on a suspended account are escalated to a human reviewer rather than
executed automatically.
