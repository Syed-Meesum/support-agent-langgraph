# Cancellation Policy

## Overview

Customers may cancel an active subscription at any time. Cancellation always
requires explicit customer confirmation before it is executed, regardless of the
plan or price. It is never performed silently from an ambiguous message.

## Effect of cancellation

Cancellation stops future renewals. Access continues until the end of the current
paid period. Amounts already charged for the current period are not automatically
refunded; a refund must be requested and assessed separately under the refund
policy.

## Confirmation requirement

Before cancelling, the customer must confirm which subscription they want cancelled.
If the request is ambiguous, ask rather than guess.

## Non-active subscriptions

Subscriptions that are already canceled, or that are in a past_due state, are
escalated for human review instead of being cancelled automatically.
