# Duplicate Charge Policy

## Definition

A duplicate charge is two or more succeeded charges for the same amount, on the
same order, occurring within a short window of each other (typically the same day,
minutes apart).

## Handling

When a duplicate charge is identified, the customer is entitled to a refund of the
duplicated amount - that is, the total of the extra charge beyond the first. The
first charge is the legitimate one and is not refunded.

## Verification

Before refunding, confirm that the charge being refunded is the duplicate and not
the original. Both charges must be succeeded and share the same order.

## Failed retries

A failed charge attempt is never billed to the customer and needs no refund. If a
failed attempt sits alongside two succeeded charges, the succeeded pair is still the
duplicate.
