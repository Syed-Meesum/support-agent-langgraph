# Refund Timing and Partial Refunds

## Partial refunds

A partial refund returns only part of a charge - for example one duplicate out of
two, or a pro-rated amount for an unused period. A partial refund is assessed under
the same thresholds as a full refund, based on the amount actually being refunded.

## Full refunds

A full refund returns the entire amount of a single charge. It is only issued when
the whole charge is in error, such as the duplicate in a duplicate-charge case.

## Timing

Refunds are recorded at the moment they are approved and issued. The record is
immediately visible in the case history. Settlement with the customer's bank takes
longer and is outside our control.

## Currency

Amounts are held and refunded in the currency of the original charge. The system
does not perform currency conversion.
