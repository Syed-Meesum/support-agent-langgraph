# Terms of Service (Excerpt)

## Section 4 - Fees and Payment

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless
cancelled before the renewal date. The customer authorises us to charge the payment
method on file for each renewal.

## Section 5 - Billing Errors

If the customer believes they were charged in error, they must notify us within 90
days of the charge. We will investigate and, where the error is confirmed, issue a
correction or refund.

## Section 6 - Refunds

Refunds are granted at our discretion and in line with the published refund policy.
Certain charges, including charges older than 90 days, may require additional
review.

## Section 7 - Account Status

We may suspend an account for non-payment or suspected abuse. While an account is
suspended, billing actions are paused and require review.
